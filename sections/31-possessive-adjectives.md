# Possessive Adjectives

**POSSESSIVE ADJECTIVES**

| Subject | Possessive adjective | English equivalent | Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Je | **mon** + (masculine singular noun)<br>**mon** + (feminine singular noun beginning with a vowel)<br>**ma** + (feminine singular noun)<br>**mes** + (masc. / fem. plural noun) | my | mon livre<br>mon amie<br>ma chambre<br>mes clés |
| Tu | **ton** + (masculine singular noun)<br>**ton** + (feminine singular noun beginning with a vowel)<br>**ta** + (feminine singular noun)<br>**tes** + (masc. / fem. plural noun) | your | ton livre<br>ton amie<br>ta chambre<br>tes clés |
| Il / Elle / On | **son** + (masculine singular noun)<br>**son** + (feminine singular noun beginning with a vowel)<br>**sa** + (feminine singular noun)<br>**ses** + (masc. / fem. plural noun) | his / her / one’s | son livre<br>son amie<br>sa chambre<br>ses clés |
| Nous | **notre** + (masculine singular noun)<br>**notre** + (feminine singular noun)<br>**nos** + (masc. / fem. plural noun) | our | notre livre<br>notre chambre<br>nos clés |
| Vous | **votre** + (masculine singular noun)<br>**votre** + (feminine singular noun)<br>**vos** + (masc. / fem. plural noun) | your | votre livre<br>votre chambre<br>vos clés |
| Ils / Elles | **leur** + (masculine singular noun)<br>**leur** + (feminine singular noun)<br>**leurs** + (masc. / fem. plural noun) | their | leur livre<br>leur chambre<br>leurs clés |

* With a feminine noun beginning with a vowel or a vowel sound, the masculine form **mon, ton,** and **son** is used instead of the feminine form in order to maintain liaison.

mon omelette  
ton amie

NOTE: Possessive adjectives agree in number (singular or plural) and gender (masculine or feminine) with the nouns they modify, which may not necessarily be the subject.

Marc a ses livres. *(Marc has his books.)*  
(*ses* agrees in gender and number with *livres*, not *Marc*.)
