# Object Pronouns

**OBJECT PRONOUNS**

a. Indirect pronouns **LUI** and **LEUR** are used to replace nouns referring to people when those nouns are preceded by the preposition **à**. There is no distinction in gender, so the meaning is understood by context. Indirect objects are placed before the conjugated verb or, when there is more than one verb, before the verb of which it is the indirect object.

| Structure and examples | English |
| :---- | :---- |
| **À + person (singular) = lui**<br>**Lui** = to him / her<br>Parlez-vous à votre prof ?<br>Oui, je lui parle souvent.<br>A-t-elle envoyé une carte postale à son amie ?<br>Qu’est-ce qu’il a donné à ses copines ?<br>Il leur a donné des disques.<br>Est-ce que vous espérez parler à vos amis ?<br>Oui, j’espère leur parler. | *Do you talk to your teacher?*<br>*Yes, I talk to him/her often.*<br>*Did she send a postcard to her friend?*<br>*What did he give to his friends?*<br>*He gave them some records.*<br>*Do you hope to talk to your friends?*<br>*Yes, I hope to talk to them.* |

B. Disjunctive (stressed) pronouns are used whenever a pronoun is needed in a stressed position—anywhere other than directly before a verb.

| French | English | French | English |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| moi | me | nous | us |
| toi | you | vous | you |
| lui | him | eux | them |
| elle | her | elles | them |

1. When referring to a person as the object of any preposition except **à**, use the disjunctive pronoun.

| French | English |
| :---- | :---- |
| Est-ce que vous parlez de Marie ?<br>Oui, nous parlons d’elle.<br>Avez-vous voyagé avec les Smith ?<br>Oui, j’ai voyagé avec eux.<br>Tu vas chez Marc ?<br>Non, je ne vais pas chez lui. | *Are you talking about Mary?*<br>*Yes, we’re talking about her.*<br>*Did you travel with the Smiths?*<br>*Yes, I traveled with them.*<br>*Are you going to Marc’s house?*<br>*No, I’m not going to his house.* |

2. After **que** in a comparison

| French | English |
| :---- | :---- |
| Tu as plus de cassettes que Paul ?<br>Non, il a plus de cassettes que moi.<br>Est-ce que vous avez les mêmes albums que nous ?<br>Oui, nous avons les mêmes albums que vous. | *Do you have more cassettes than Paul?*<br>*No, he has more cassettes than me.*<br>*Do you have the same albums as us?*<br>*Yes, we have the same albums as you.* |

3. As a pronoun standing alone

| French | English |
| :---- | :---- |
| Qui a acheté ce disque ?<br>Lui.<br>Alors, qui l’a choisi ?<br>Moi. | *Who bought this record?*<br>*Him.*<br>*Well, who chose it?*<br>*Me.* |

4. For emphasizing the subject

| French | English |
| :---- | :---- |
| Et toi, comment as-tu trouvé le concert ?<br>Moi, je l’ai beaucoup aimé, mais lui, il l’a détesté. | *And you, how did you like the concert?*<br>*I really liked it, but he hated it.* |

C. Pronouns **Y** and **EN**

**Y** replaces:
1. a preposition of location (**à, en, sur, chez, dans, sous, devant**, etc.) + object
2. **à** + noun referring to things (not people)
3. **à** + verbs in the infinitive referring to ideas

| French | Replacement |
| :---- | :---- |
| Je vais chez des amis.<br>Le livre se trouve sur la table.<br>Je m’intéresse aux sports.<br>Il a répondu à la question.<br>Nous nous intéressons à partir. | J’y vais.<br>Le livre s’y trouve.<br>Je m’y intéresse.<br>Il y a répondu.<br>Nous nous y intéressons. |

**EN** replaces:
1. **de** + noun
2. **de** + verb

| French | Replacement |
| :---- | :---- |
| J’ai besoin d’un livre.<br>Il mange de la viande.<br>Tu as peur de chanter.<br>Il a deux livres.<br>Elle n’a pas beaucoup d’argent. | J’en ai besoin.<br>Il en mange.<br>Tu en as peur.<br>Il en a deux.<br>Elle n’en a pas beaucoup. |

| Note |
| :---: |
| *Nouns preceded by numbers or expressions of quantity are replaced, but not the number or expression of quantity.* |

D. Direct object pronouns **LE, LA, LES**

The direct object pronouns **le**, **la**, and **les** replace a noun object that directly follows the verb without being preceded by a preposition. The pronoun agrees in gender and number with the noun it replaces. The object pronoun is placed directly before the conjugated verb. However, when the verb in the present or past is followed by an infinitive, the object pronoun must directly precede the infinitive.

| French | English |
| :---- | :---- |
| Aimez-vous le football ?<br>Oui, je l’aime bien.<br>Regardez-vous les matches de foot à la télé ?<br>Oui, je les regarde souvent. | *Do you like soccer?*<br>*Yes, I really like it.*<br>*Do you watch soccer matches on TV?*<br>*Yes, I watch them often.* |

| French | English |
| :---- | :---- |
| Avez-vous vu le match d’hier ?<br>Non, je ne l’ai pas vu.<br>Aimez-vous faire la lessive ?<br>Non, je n’aime pas la faire. | *Did you see yesterday’s match?*<br>*No, I didn’t see it.*<br>*Do you like to do the wash?*<br>*No, I don’t like to do it.* |

When the object pronouns **le**, **la**, and **les** precede the auxiliary verb **avoir** in the *passé composé*, the past participle agrees in gender and number with this preceding direct object.

| French | English |
| :---- | :---- |
| Avez-vous vu la femme dans l’équipe de foot ?<br>Oui, je l’ai vue.<br>Vous avez regardé les matches à la télé ?<br>Oui, je les ai regardés et je les ai beaucoup aimés.<br>Avez-vous vu Sophie et Anne au match de tennis ?<br>Oui, je les ai vues. | *Did you see the woman on the soccer team?*<br>*Yes, I saw her.*<br>*Did you watch the games on TV?*<br>*Yes, I watched them and I really liked them.*<br>*Did you see Sophie and Anne at the tennis match?*<br>*Yes, I saw them.* |

| Subject | Reflexive | Direct Object | Indirect Object | Disjunctive (stressed) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| je | me | me | me | moi |
| tu | te | te | te | toi |
| il / elle / on | se | le / la / le | lui | lui / elle / soi |
| nous | nous | nous | nous | nous |
| vous | vous | vous | vous | vous |
| ils / elles | se | les | leur | eux / elles |

**THE ORDER OF OBJECT PRONOUNS**

| me, te, se, nous, vous | le, la, les | lui, leur | y | en |
